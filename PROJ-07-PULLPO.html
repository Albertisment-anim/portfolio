Graphic design and animation

In 2022 and 2023 I got the chance to work alongside Pullpo's co-founder, Marco Patiño, to bring his vision to life. I designed the logo for the brand, and later on, I animated a teaser video for the launch of the product. This video was scripted, storyboarded, designed and animated by me. Here is the final result:

The video was animated entirely with After Effects, while the storyboard was done in Blender. Check out the process behind the logo below. It involved drawing a lot of octopuses, sketching even more thumbnails and a long path of iterations, trial and error and consulting with Marco.

The most demanding part was, however, giving life to the final logo, wich involved designing the motion with hand-drawn animation, rigging the vectorized design and a lot of frame by frame tweaking. Worth it don't you think? Isn't it cute?
