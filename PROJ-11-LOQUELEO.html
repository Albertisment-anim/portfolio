Loqueleo (2020-2022)
>

Co-direction, storyboarding, illustration and animation

From 2020-2022 I worked creating animated booktrailers and digital content for the social media channels of Loqueleo, the children's catalogue of publishing company Santillana. It was an opportunity to play around with different styles, techniques and tools, like mixing 3D and 2D in Blender. It also gave me the chance to work on these videos from script and concept to animating and editing the final piece, which was really formative.

The first two videos above were made in Linkvids with additional text animation and motion graphics by Carlos Blanco. The other two as well as the two below were made alongside Eva Durán, who edited the videos and made the sound design.
